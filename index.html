Building the society back up, one committee at a time.

CESS is the student society of the Civil Engineering Department. Founded to strengthen academic life, professional growth, and community among civil engineering students, our activities paused during the war — a new committee is now rebuilding it.

Why CESS exists

A society run by civil engineering students, for civil engineering students.

Academic

Strengthening academic life

Study sessions, revision material, and peer support that connect classroom learning with practical understanding.

Professional

Preparing future engineers

Training, workshops, and exposure to the tools and standards practiced in the civil engineering profession.

Community

A department that supports itself

Community service and department-wide events that keep students connected across cohorts.

Continuity

Institutional memory

A structured archive and handover system so each new committee builds on the last, not from zero.
